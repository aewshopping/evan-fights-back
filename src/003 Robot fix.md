"Right," said Evan, "let's get this robot working."

He picked up the golden droid and placed it on the kitchen table. His brother, Arthur, came over to watch.

"You'll never get it working," Arthur said smugly. "You're rubbish with robots."

"Am not!" Evan retorted. "I just need to figure out how it works."

He looked the robot over. It was smooth and shiny, with no obvious buttons or switches. He ran his hand over its head and felt a small, silver button tucked away behind its neck. That was interesting.

Then he saw a small seam running around the robot's chest. It looked like a panel that could be opened.

Finally, he noticed its belly button. It wasn't a normal belly button, it was a small hole that looked just the right size for...

"Are you going to use your new screwdriver set?" asked Dad, walking into the room.

"Yeah!" said Evan. He now had a few ideas. What should he try first?
