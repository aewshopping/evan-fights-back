You decide that the robot can wait. You've got a huge pile of presents to open, and you can't wait to see what's inside. You rip open the wrapping paper on the biggest present. It's a brand new football.

"Wow," you say. "Thanks Dad."

"No problem," says Dad. "Let's go and have a kick about in the garden."

You and Dad go outside and play football. You score three goals and Dad scores one. You're having a great time, but you can't stop thinking about the robot. What if it's broken? What if you can't get it to work?

After a while, you get tired of playing football. "I'm going to go and have a look at that robot," you say.
