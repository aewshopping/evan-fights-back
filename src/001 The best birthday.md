"Happy birthday Evan," said Dad, ruffling his hair. "I've got a very special present for you."

Evan ripped open the box. Inside was a small golden robot, about the same size as him. Its eyes were two large, round lights.

"Wow," said Evan. "A robot! Thanks Dad."

"I found him in a second-hand droid shop," said Dad. "He's a bit old, but the man in the shop said he was one of a kind."

Evan looked at the robot. It was beautiful, but it didn't seem to be working. There were no lights on, and it was completely still.

"Is it broken?" asked Evan.

"I don't think so," said Dad. "The man in the shop said it just needed charging."

Evan's older brother Arthur came into the room. "Wow, a robot!" he said. "Can I have a go?"

"No," said Evan, hugging the robot. "He's mine."

"Don't be selfish," said Arthur. "Let me see."

"There are lots of other presents to open," said Dad, "what do you want to do Evan?"
