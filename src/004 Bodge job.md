Evan ignored the button and the chest panel. The belly button looked far more interesting. He picked the smallest screwdriver from his new set.

"The belly button? Really?" said Arthur, rolling his eyes. "You're going to break it."

"I know what I'm doing," said Evan, although he wasn't so sure. He carefully inserted the screwdriver into the small hole.

There was a loud *clunk* and a *whirring* sound. Suddenly, the robot's shiny golden bottom fell off and clattered onto the table.

Arthur burst out laughing. "You broke him! His bum fell off!"

Evan's face went red. "I didn't break him!" He quickly picked up the robot's bottom. It looked like it just clicked back into place. With a nervous glance at his brother, he snapped it back on. It fit perfectly.

"See? Good as new," he said, trying to look confident. "That wasn't the right button, that's all."
