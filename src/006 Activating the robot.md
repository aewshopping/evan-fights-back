Evan had one last hope: the small, silver button on the back of the robot's neck. He'd ignored it until now, but it felt like his last chance.

"This is it," he said, his voice a determined whisper. Arthur leaned in close, a skeptical look on his face.

With a deep breath, Evan pressed the button.

The robot's eyes lit up, not with the flickering blue of before, but with a warm, steady, golden light. A soft whirring sound filled the room. The robot straightened up, its little golden body humming with energy.

"Greetings, Master," it said, its voice a surprising mix of posh and camp. "I am B9-QT1, a class three serving droid from the Gamma Quadrant. It is an honour to be of service."

Evan and Arthur stared, their mouths wide open.

"He's... he's working!" stammered Evan, a huge grin spreading across his face.

The robot, B9-QT1, took a small, mincing step forward. "I am indeed, Master. Although, I must say, my posterior plating feels a trifle... loose."

Arthur snorted with laughter. Evan just beamed. This was the best birthday present ever.
