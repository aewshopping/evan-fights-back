"Let's try this then," said Evan, pointing the screwdriver at the robot's chest. He carefully prized open the panel. Inside, a tangle of multi-coloured wires greeted him. It looked like a plate of spaghetti.

"You don't know what you're doing," whispered Arthur, leaning over his shoulder. "You'll break it for good."

"Will not!" said Evan. He gently prodded a bright blue wire with the tip of his screwdriver.

The robot's eyes flickered on, flashing a bright, electric blue. A voice, high-pitched and tinny, crackled from its speaker.

"Glibber-globber-flobber-wobber!" it squeaked. Then its eyes went dark and it fell silent again.

"See?" said Arthur. "You've made it talk nonsense. You've definitely broken it now."

Evan felt a bit disappointed. He carefully closed the chest panel. "It just needs a proper fix," he said. "I'll get it." But he was starting to have his doubts.
